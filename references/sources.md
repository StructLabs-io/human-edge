# Sources

Every source behind this kit, grouped by theme, newest first within each group. Each entry gives a date, the authors, the title, where it was published and a link.

## How these were checked

The research was gathered in October 2026 by AI research agents, each required to open primary sources. A separate audit then checked about 40 of the sources against the original papers, favouring every number this kit or its talk quotes. None was fabricated. Sources added after the audit (the Malaysia data, and a few studies the audit said were missing) were checked for the right title, author and year but not re-audited in full. Every link below was checked to point to the right item.

**Tags:**
- `full`: read in full, or the official page or report itself.
- `abstract`: read through the abstract, a university release, PubMed or a citation index, because the full text was paywalled.
- `coverage`: known only through reputable press coverage.
- `preprint`: not yet peer-reviewed.
- `vendor`: published by a company that sells AI or advisory services.

Corrections are welcome as GitHub issues.
