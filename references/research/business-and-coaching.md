# Business and coaching: what clients say they still want from people

The evidence behind `../business-audit.md`. Most of it is *stated preference* (what people say in surveys), not proof of what they'll pay for. Vendor data is marked *vendor*.

## 1. What clients say they still want, ranked by strength of evidence

1. **A person accountable for the outcome.**
   - **Law.** The EU AI Act requires high-risk AI to be overseen by people who can override or stop it ([Art. 14](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/14/)). Lawyers may not rely on AI output without appropriate independent review ([ABA Formal Opinion 512, 2024](https://www.americanbar.org/content/dam/aba/administrative/professional_responsibility/ethics-opinions/aba-formal-opinion-512.pdf)).
   - **Customers.** 45% are more likely to use an AI agent that has a clear path to a human ([Salesforce, 2024](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/ai-customer-research/), N = 15,015; *vendor*).
   - **Service leaders.** 95% of 163 service leaders plan to keep human agents (*vendor*; [Gartner, 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-10-gartner-predicts-50-percent-of-organizations-will-abandon-plans-to-reduce-customer-service-workforce-due-to-ai)). Gartner predicts that by 2027 half of organisations will abandon plans to cut customer-service staff because of AI.
2. **Judgment about what fits *us*.**
   - **B2B buyers.** 61% prefer a rep-free buying experience overall, yet want sellers for decisions that need "contextual intelligence" (*vendor*; [Gartner, 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience), N = 632).
   - **AI users.** Users say AI "lacks the judgment, contextual awareness, and situational reasoning that their work requires" ([Anthropic, survey of about 9,700 users, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report); *vendor*).
   - **B2B buying with AI.** [Forrester's 2026 State of Business Buying](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/01/22/forrester-b2b-buying-ai-2026/) (coverage) looks at how AI is changing business buying.
3. **Trust from someone they know.**
   - **Customer service.** 64% of customers would prefer companies not use AI in customer service, and 53% would consider switching to a competitor. Their top fear is losing access to a person (*vendor*; [Gartner, 2024](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-07-09-gartner-survey-finds-64-percent-of-customers-would-prefer-that-companies-didnt-use-ai-for-customer-service), N = 5,728, fielded December 2023).
   - **Trust is turning local.** 70% are hesitant to trust people unlike them, and employees trust their own employer at 78% ([Edelman Trust Barometer 2026](https://www.edelman.com/news-awards/2026-edelman-trust-barometer-society-slides-into-insularity), N ≈ 34,000, 28 countries). That measures insularity, not a preference for humans over AI.
   - **"Someone like me".** 78% of Americans trust "someone like me" on AI ([Edelman AI Flash Poll, 2025](https://www.edelman.com/sites/g/files/aatuss191/files/2025-11/AI%20Flash%20Poll_Top%2010%20Findings_White.pdf)).
4. **Field-tested expertise and the ability to steer AI.**
   - **Experienced workers.** The experience gradient in AI-exposed jobs survives an education control ([Brynjolfsson, Chandar & Chen, 2026](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf)).
   - **Claude Code users** (*vendor*). Those who signed up six or more months earlier succeed 10% more often ([Anthropic, March 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)). Success comes "more from command of a domain than from the ability to write code" ([Anthropic, June 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise)).
5. **Leading and developing people.**
   - **Employers.** Leadership and social influence is a core skill for 61% of employers, and skills "rooted in human interaction" show "no substitution potential" ([WEF Future of Jobs, 2025](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf)).
   - **Bottlenecks.** When automation leaves one human bottleneck in a process, that bottleneck gains value; David Deming calls management skills measurable and underpriced ([Deming, 2026](https://thecontextwindowpodcast.substack.com/p/how-social-skills-can-get-you-paid)).
   - **Leading AI and leading people.** Skill at leading AI agents correlated 0.81 with skill at leading human teams ([Weidmann, Xu & Deming, NBER, 2025](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33662)).
   - **Interpersonal skills.** "Interpersonal skills like negotiation and coaching may change the least" ([McKinsey Global Institute, 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/agents-robots-and-us-skill-partnerships-in-the-age-of-ai)).
   - **Skills on the rise.** [LinkedIn Skills on the Rise 2026](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/Skills-on-the-rise-2026) tracks the skills employers are hiring for.
6. **Commitment inside a coaching relationship.**
   - **Trials.**
     - Human coaching beat a scripted AI chatbot on goal attainment and stress ([de Haan, Terblanche & Nowack, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1080/13678868.2026.2633990), N = 114).
     - Human coaches scored higher on insight, goals, commitment and alliance, with no difference on trust or confidentiality ([Passmore et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1108/JWAM-02-2025-0032), n = 63, not randomized).
   - **Preference.** BetterUp says 34% of its users prefer a human coach, 15% AI and 51% both (company figures with no method given; [BetterUp, 2025](https://www.betterup.com/press/betterup-launches-ai-coaching); *vendor*).
   - **Market size.** The coaching industry is US$5.34 billion with 122,974 coaches, with emerging markets driving growth ([ICF Global Coaching Study 2025, released 2026](https://coachingfederation.org/blog/global-coaching-industry-surpasses-5-34-billion-as-emerging-markets-drive-growth/)).
7. **Taste and agency.**
   - **Practitioners.** Mollick, Godin and Katzenberg back them (see `voices.md`).
   - **Microsoft's 2026 data.** Quality control of AI output is the top-ranked human skill (50%), with critical thinking second (46%) ([Work Trend Index 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization); *vendor*).
   - **Research.** Thin.

## 2. Data that cuts against the "human skills premium"

- **Service and sales are highly AI-applicable.** Customer-service and sales tasks rank among the most AI-applicable activities (*vendor*: Microsoft; [Tomlinson et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07935)).
- **AI out-scores people on the written work.** It beats people on rated compassion and on persuasion (`ai-already-matches.md`).
- **AI flattens the experience gap in some jobs.** Support agents gained 14% on average and 34% for novices, with little gain for veterans ([Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, *QJE*, 2025](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161)).
- **Pay doesn't reflect it yet.** Interpersonal skills are "not currently prioritized in wage-based evaluations" ([Shao et al., Stanford, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06576)).
- **Canaries is descriptive.** Its patterns weaken when controlling for education, some trends predate generative AI, and the authors call the findings descriptive, not causal.
- **Klarna reversed course.** It reported quality problems after leaning hard on AI for support and brought humans back ([Fortune, 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/05/09/klarna-ai-humans-return-on-investment/); coverage). This is one company's account.

## 3. How people actually use AI at work

- **What ChatGPT is used for.** Most ChatGPT use is for practical guidance, information and writing, and a growing share is non-work (*vendor*: OpenAI co-authors; [Chatterji, Deming et al., NBER, 2025](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34255)).
- **Anthropic's own data** (*vendor*):
  - The Economic Index tracks which tasks people bring to Claude, and how much they delegate versus collaborate ([January 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report); [March 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report); [June 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)).
  - [What 81,000 people told us (2026)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/81k-economics) reports a large survey of how people feel about AI and work.
- **Doctors.** Two in three US physicians used health AI in 2024, up from 38% in 2023 ([AMA, 2025](https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital-health/2-3-physicians-are-using-health-ai-78-2023)).
- **The US public.** Americans are more concerned than excited about AI's role in daily life, and want more control over it ([Pew Research Center, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2025/09/17/how-americans-view-ai-and-its-impact-on-people-and-society/)).

## 4. Malaysia and Southeast Asia: how far this applies

Most of the evidence above comes from the US, UK and EU. Points that change the picture for Malaysian and regional businesses:

- **Trust in AI is higher in emerging economies.** Across 47 countries, 57% of people in emerging economies were willing to trust AI systems, versus 39% in advanced economies (46% globally) ([KPMG and University of Melbourne, 2025](https://kpmg.com/za/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025/05/global-study-reveals-trust-of-ai-remains-a-critical-challenge-reflecting-tension-between-benefits-and-risks.html); [report](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2025/05/trust-attitudes-and-use-of-ai-global-report.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). **Malaysia was not in that survey**, so don't quote it as a Malaysian figure. (The report is licensed CC BY-NC-SA; this kit cites its numbers but doesn't reproduce its charts.)
- **Malaysian data shows high comfort with AI, on related measures** ([Ipsos AI Monitor 2026](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2026-06/AI%20Monitor%202026%20%28English%29.pdf); Malaysia n = 500, adults 18–74, fieldwork March–April 2026):

  | Statement (% agree) | Malaysia | Global |
  |---|---|---|
  | "I trust AI tools, so I don't check their work" | 37% | 31% (Asia 40%, US 23%) |
  | AI tools have saved me time at work in the last year | 82% | 62% |
  | AI will change how I do my job in the next five years (% likely) | 84% | 62% |
  | Products and services using AI make me excited | 76% | 51% |
  | AI products and services have more benefits than drawbacks | 70% | 55% |
  | Trust AI not to discriminate / trust *people* not to discriminate | 64% / 61% | 55% / 47% |

  In the 2025 edition, 73% of Malaysians trusted the government to regulate AI responsibly (54% globally), and 63% worried AI could replace their job within three to five years ([Ipsos AI Monitor 2025](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos%20Press%20Release_Ipsos%20AI%20Monitor%202025_240625.pdf)).
- **What this means for the human premium.** Malaysians are more excited about AI, more likely to say it saves them time at work, and more willing to skip checking it. Two consequences:
  - Customers here may accept more automation than US surveys suggest.
  - Over-trust (Edge 4 in `edges.md`) is a bigger risk here, not a smaller one.
- **Trust is high in business and employers.** In Malaysia, 76% trust business and 82% of employees trust their own employer, and Malaysia ranks 7th of 28 countries on Edelman's Trust Index ([Edelman Trust Barometer 2026, Malaysia report](https://www.edelman.com/sites/g/files/aatuss191/files/my/2026-05/2026%20Edelman%20Trust%20Barometer_Malaysia%20Report.pdf)). Edelman asked Malaysians nothing about AI.
- **Accountability rules.** Malaysia's [National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/my-initiative/whole-government-digital-services/tadbir-urus/national-guidelines-on-artificial-intelligence-governance-and-ethics-aige) were launched on 20 September 2024 and are voluntary. Of their seven principles:
  - Principle 6 says developers, owners and AI actors "should be accountable for the success or failure of the AI solutions".
  - Principle 7 names human oversight: human-in-the-loop, human-on-the-loop and human-in-command ([full guidelines](https://www.mosti.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NATIONAL-GUIDELINES-OF-AIGE-20241118.pdf)).
  - The EU AI Act doesn't apply here.
  - As of October 2026, no binding AI law had been passed or tabled; an AI Governance Bill went to public consultation in July 2026 ([Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/07/malaysia-public-consultation-on-the-ai-governance-bill)) and was expected in Parliament in early 2027 ([The Edge, 5 October 2026](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/820491)). Check its status before quoting this.
- **Power distance and face.** Malaysia scores 100 on The Culture Factor's version of Hofstede's power-distance index, among the highest of any country: "subordinates expect direction" ([The Culture Factor](https://www.theculturefactor.com/country-comparison-tool?countries=malaysia)). Honest challenge is harder to give and lands differently. In our experience (not a research finding), bad news, negotiations, collections and apologies usually belong with a person, even when AI could write the words.
- **Asian samples worth knowing:**
  - Strömberg et al.: 26,811 Chinese students, the learning penalty.
  - Yan and Liu et al.: the two "human first" trials, both in China.
  - Agarwal et al.: AI pulled Indian writers toward Western styles.
- **Also outside the US and EU:** Bastani et al.'s field trial was in Turkey.
- **Not covered by this research.** Malaysia-specific studies on AI in coaching or customer service, and non-English sources.
